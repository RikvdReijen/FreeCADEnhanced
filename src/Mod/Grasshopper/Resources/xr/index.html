Experimental FreeCAD Grasshopper mode

A node canvas that lies on your table, anchored to a printed QR marker. Tap nodes with the Logitech MX Ink (or a fingertip), drag wires, slide the sheet with a flat hand. Geometry previews float beside the sheet and every edit shows up in FreeCAD immediately.

WebXR needs a secure context: open this page via http://localhost:<port>/xr through adb reverse on the headset, or configure a certificate in FreeCAD's Grasshopper preferences.
